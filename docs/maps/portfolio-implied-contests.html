Portfolio-Implied Contests: literature map

The paper Portfolio-Implied Contests, with an Application to Tail Risk reads a long-only portfolio as the winning probabilities of a race, and uses that reading to adjust a benchmark for a view about dependence. The adjustment is Thurstone portfolio polishing. This map places both among the work they draw on and the work they differ from.

Each node is a paper. Colours are literatures. Hover a node or an edge for a one-line summary, click a node to open the reference, and drag to rearrange.

Where the method sits

  • Reverse optimization recovers expected returns from a portfolio, with the covariance given. Polishing recovers abilities, with the dependence given, and then changes the dependence.
  • Entropy pooling and copula versions of Black–Litterman accept views about tails. They re-weight or simulate scenarios and then solve a program for the weights.
  • Share inversion in the choice literature is closed form for the logit and iterative for correlated tastes. The inverse used here is deterministic for independent, factor, group, nested and tree dependence.
  • Hierarchical and scenario methods reach the tail without inverting a covariance. They carry no benchmark anchor and no inverse map.

Open questions in the paper

  • The choice of reference law and of the strength of the view.
  • Estimation error in the abilities, a counterpart of covariance shrinkage.
  • A robust online estimate of downside covariance to drive the race.
  • Whether a tail view improves drawdown or expected shortfall out of sample.
