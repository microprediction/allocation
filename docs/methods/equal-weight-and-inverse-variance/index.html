Equal weight

wi = 1 / n

Uses no estimate at all. Its only trades restore the weights after prices move.

The streaming version spreads the weight over the assets that have been observed at least min_obs times, so a newly listed asset does not receive a full share on its first day.

Inverse variance

wi ∝ 1 / σi2

The minimum-variance portfolio when correlations are ignored. It depends only on the diagonal of the covariance.

It is the default benchmark of Thurstone portfolio polishing and the near end of the Schur bridge inverse_variance_to_min_variance.

Use

from allocation import EqualWeight, InverseVariance

EqualWeight().fit(returns).weights_
InverseVariance(halflife=120).fit(returns).weights_
