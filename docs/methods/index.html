Methods

Every estimator in the package, and what each one is for.

Each method has a batch class with fit, partial_fit and weights_, and a streaming class with learn_one and predict_one for a universe whose members change. The two interfaces are described in Streaming and changing universes.

Choosing

  • You hold a benchmark and have a view about which assets fall together. Thurstone portfolio polishing.
  • You want something between hierarchical risk parity and minimum variance. Schur bridge, or Schur-complementary if you need to match skfolio.
  • More assets than observations. Methods that need no full inverse: Thurstone with factors=k, hierarchical risk parity, inverse variance, or FactorMinimumVariance.
  • Long-only weights are required. Use a method marked long-only above, or wrap a signed one in BoxConstrained.
