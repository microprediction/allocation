Risk parity

Every asset contributes the same share of portfolio risk.

Batch
RiskParity
Streaming
StreamingRiskParity
Weights
Strictly positive, sum to one
Covariance inverse
None

Asset i's contribution to portfolio variance is wi(Σw)i. Risk parity, also called equal risk contribution, chooses the weights that make these equal.

What it does

The solution is the unique strictly positive solution of a convex problem. No weight is ever at a bound, so the weights are a smooth function of the covariance.

It is computed by coordinate descent. Each update starts from the previous weights, which speeds convergence and keeps successive solutions close.

Unequal targets are allowed: pass budgets to give each asset a chosen share of risk.

Use

from allocation import RiskParity

RiskParity().fit(returns).weights_

RiskParity(budgets=shares).fit(returns).weights_   # one share per asset

Parameters

ParameterDefaultMeaning
budgetsNoneRisk share per asset. None is equal shares.
covariance_estimatorNoneAny online estimator with partial_fit and covariance_. None uses an exponentially weighted covariance.

Background

  • Maillard, Roncalli and Teïletche (2010), The Properties of Equally Weighted Risk Contribution Portfolios
  • The function allocation.baselines.risk_parity_weights(covariance, budgets) returns the weights for a given covariance.
