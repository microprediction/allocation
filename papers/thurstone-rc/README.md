# Thurstone Portfolios — FMF Rapid Communication

Condensed from `papers/thurstone-portfolios/` for the *Frontiers of
Mathematical Finance* **Rapid Communications** track
(https://www.aimsciences.org/FMF/RapidCommunications).

## Track requirements (as published, July 2026)

- Original and significant research; concise and self-contained; sufficient
  mathematical or empirical evidence for the principal results.
- **Main text ≤ 5 pages. Figures, tables, and references ≤ 4 additional pages.**
- Expedited handling, rigorous peer review, target decision in 4–5 weeks.
- Publication as a Rapid Communication does not preclude a later, substantially
  expanded article (the full working paper remains publishable).
- Editorial contact for Rapid Communications: Xin Guo (xinguo@berkeley.edu).

Current layout is compliant: pages 1–5 main text (title/abstract included),
page 6 references, page 7 tables, page 8 figure.

## Submission (per FMF Guide for Authors)

- PDF by email to **samfinance@umd.edu**, stating the editor the paper should
  be directed to — for this track, **Xin Guo** — and the article type
  **Rapid Communication**.
- Required: full author name, email, **postal address and affiliation**
  (placeholder footnote in `paper.tex` — fill before sending), keywords, and
  JEL codes (present: G11, C25, C15, C58; MSC2020 also given).
- Copyright stays with the author; CC BY 4.0 on acceptance. No length limit
  applies journal-wide, but the RC limits above bind for this track.
